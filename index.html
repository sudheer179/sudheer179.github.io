About me

I'm a senior front-end developer with 10+ years of experience, mostly in React and Angular, plus Vue and React Native. I've worked on healthcare, retail, and insurance products, and accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA) has been a constant in all of them.

Day to day that means TypeScript, Redux or NgRx, component libraries in Storybook, and wiring UIs to REST and GraphQL APIs. I'm comfortable going into the Node.js or Spring Boot layer when the API isn't giving the front end what it needs, and I've shipped through CI/CD on AWS, Azure, and GCP. I also use Claude AI and GitHub Copilot for scaffolding, refactors, and test writing.
